### Recording identification method

Compared the ten bundled waveforms against 32 candidate original files from the [University of York recording archive](https://webfiles.york.ac.uk/OPENAIR/IRs/), using resampling, correlation and channel fitting. The selected matches explain over 99.9% of bundled signal energy across the common duration. This identifies modified recordings rather than claiming byte identity. The inventory records both original and bundled hashes and formats. Source HTML capture hashes document the exact evidence examined; publisher archive links are provided for independent inspection.

### Original stops-0.3.0: 49 exact matches

The inventory identifies **49 byte-for-byte matches** to the [original stops-0.3.0 source archive](https://deb.debian.org/debian/pool/main/s/stops/stops_0.3.0.orig.tar.gz). The upstream archive does not itself include a COPYING file. The [Debian 0.3.0-3 copyright record](https://sources.debian.org/src/stops/0.3.0-3/debian/copyright/) applies **GPL version 2 or any later version** to the upstream files and records **Copyright 2005–2007 Fons Adriaensen <fons.adriaensen@skynet.be>**. We verified that record inside the matching [Debian packaging archive](https://deb.debian.org/debian/pool/main/s/stops/stops_0.3.0-3.debian.tar.xz), separately from the upstream model data. Both archive hashes are in the inventory.

Retain that grant and disclaimer in [Aeolus-stops-notice.txt](../LICENSES/Aeolus-stops-notice.txt), [GPLv2](../LICENSES/GPL-2.0.txt), and the models' individual author/comment fields. The later-version option permits using GPLv3 with this application; the inherited GPL-2.0-or-later grant is preserved, not rewritten as GPLv2-only.

Individual embedded credits include **Fons Adriaensen** (`FA`/`fa`), **Matthias Nagorni** (spelled out in `III_principal_8.ae0`, plus `mn` fields), and **Nathan Hurst, modified by Fons Adriaensen** (`I_melodia.ae0`). `new_oboe_fa.ae0` retains `fa+rj` and its comment **“Based on samples by Reiner Janke”**. Preserve that sample-source credit; this file contains synthesis parameters, not a bundled sample recording. `II_flautodolce4.ae0` has an empty copyright field, which this audit does not invent or overwrite. `trombone.ae0` also retains “Based on Thomaskirche recording”. These comments describe the models' provenance; this inventory grants no rights to independently obtained source recordings. Package-level credit does not replace these individual notices.
